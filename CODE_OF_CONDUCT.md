# Code of Conduct

QuantMaster is a place to help people learn. We want everyone who takes part
(learners, teachers, contributors and maintainers) to feel welcome and safe.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners and with people whose first
  language is not English.
- Critique ideas and work, never people. Assume good intent.
- Give credit where it is due, and accept corrections gracefully.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language, in public or private.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or threats of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or using the project to advertise unrelated services.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, use GitHub's **Report content** option on
the comment, issue or pull request, or ask a maintainer for a private channel by
opening a short issue (without details). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block
people who do not follow this code.

This code is adapted from the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
