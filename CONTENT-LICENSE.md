## What it covers

Everything under `src/data/`: topics, concept lessons, questions and their
options, answers, explanations, solution steps, hints and common mistakes,
formula and shortcut cards, exam-strategy cards and achievements. It also covers
the wording that question generators (`src/generators/templates/`) produce.

## What it lets you do

You may copy, share, translate and adapt the content for any purpose, including
commercially (for example in a coaching class, a book or another app), as long as
you:

1. **Give credit:** name "QuantMaster" and link to
   https://github.com/SMACAcademy/quantmaster, say that the content is under
   CC BY-SA 4.0, and indicate if you changed it.
2. **Share alike:** if you publish an adaptation, publish it under CC BY-SA 4.0
   (or a compatible licence), so it stays open for the next learner.

A suggested credit line:

> Questions adapted from QuantMaster (https://github.com/SMACAcademy/quantmaster),
> licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Exam names

Names of examinations and their conducting bodies (IBPS, SBI, SSC, CAT and
others) are used only to describe the patterns the practice material follows.
QuantMaster is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them. The questions are
original; none are copied from past papers.
